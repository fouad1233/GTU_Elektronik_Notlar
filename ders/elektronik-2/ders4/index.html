This expression has a simple and elegant interpretation. The CG transistor Q_2 raises the output resistance of the amplifier by the factor (g_m2r_o2) , which is its intrinsic gain. At the same time, the CG transistor simply passes the current (g_m1v_i) to the output node. Thus the CG or cascode transistor very effectively the objectives we set for the current buffer with K = A_o2 = g_m2r_o2 .
