All possible root locations of a system

→ For higher order systems the ``degree'' of polynomial in the characteristic equation is high!

Finding root becomes difficult

By changing `k' roots of the characteristic equation change from `poles' to `zero' of the `open loop' T.F.

Vector representation in complex plane

origin of complex plane to s' .

F(s)=s+b for any complex s , we'll end up to as a complex number

at any s , the function value is a complex number (in general) which has a ``magnitude'' and a ``phase''

4 We need some criteria to check if the trial points ( s ) on the complex plane can be accepted as root of the characteristic equation

Any point like ` s ' which satisfies both conditions would be our point of interest (root of characteristic eq)

The locus of points satisfying the phase condition constitutes the root locus plot of the system.

Steps of Drawing

2- Check the validity of point on `Re' axis

if the number of poles and zeros are not the same

Determine the breaking points

Branches leave (break out) or enter (break in) the real axis at 90^°
