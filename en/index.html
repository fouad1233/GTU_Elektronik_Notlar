    FAQ

    Where can I find GTU Electronics Engineering lecture notes?

    This site has a page for every course from semester 1 to semester 7. Open a course to reach its lecture notes, slides, past exams, homework, lab sheets and projects. The files live in the open GitHub repository and are free to download.

    Are there past exams?

    Yes. Many courses list their midterm, final and quiz questions in the “Past exams” section of the course page. These materials belong to the instructors and Gebze Technical University and are shared for educational purposes only.

    What are the digital notes, and can I trust them?

    They are searchable, handwriting-style PDF versions of handwritten tablet notes, retyped in LaTeX (204 so far). They were created with the help of Claude (Anthropic) and may contain errors, so always check the original notes and the official course materials. Every digital note links to the scanned handwritten pages.

    Which courses have digital notes?

    For example Electromagnetic Field Theory, Analog Communication Systems, Signals and Systems, Control Systems, RF System Design for Communications, Digital Communications and others. See the digital notes page for the full list.

    Are the notes in English?

    Most notes and exams are written in Turkish, with technical terms often in English. Course names, summaries and the navigation of this site are available in English, and the titles of the digital notes are translated into English.

    I found a mistake or something is missing — how can I contribute?

    Open an issue or send a pull request on GitHub. You can reach out the same way if you would like to share your own notes.
