Semester 5 Courses

5. Dönem · 2023–2024 Güz — Gebze Teknik Üniversitesi, Elektronik Mühendisliği

GTU Electronics Engineering semester 5 (Fall 2023–24): 7 courses. Open a course to see its lecture notes, past exams, homework and projects.

Analog Communication Systems ELM361

Fourier analysis, amplitude modulation (AM, DSB, SSB), angle modulation (FM, PM), receivers and noise in communication systems.

12 lecture notes · 4 recitation notes · 22 digital notes · 10 past exams · 8 exam-prep files · 2 assignments · 2 projects

Electromagnetic Field Theory ELM341

Vector analysis, electrostatics (Coulomb's and Gauss's laws, potential, boundary conditions), magnetostatics and Maxwell's equations.

15 lecture notes · 9 recitation notes · 27 digital notes · 8 past exams · 3 exam-prep files · 1 project

Electromechanical Energy Conversion

Magnetic circuits, transformers, principles of electromechanical energy conversion, DC and AC machines.

1 digital note · 1 exam-prep file

Electronics II

Multistage and differential amplifiers, current mirrors, frequency response, feedback and output stages.

12 lecture notes · 13 digital notes · 1 exam-prep file

Electronics Laboratory II ELEC337

Low- and high-frequency behaviour of single-BJT amplifiers, LTspice simulations, the lab final and a differential-amplifier project.

3 labs · 1 project

Microprocessors ELEC334

ARM Cortex-M0+ architecture and the STM32G0 microcontroller: programming in assembly and C, memory and peripherals, interrupts and timers.

12 slide decks · 3 past exams · 3 assignments

Microprocessors Laboratory ELEC335

Assembly and C experiments on the STM32G0 Nucleo board (e.g. GPIO, UART) and lab reports.

5 labs · 1 code file
