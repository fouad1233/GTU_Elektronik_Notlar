Semester 6 Courses

6. Dönem · 2023–2024 Bahar — Gebze Teknik Üniversitesi, Elektronik Mühendisliği

GTU Electronics Engineering semester 6 (Spring 2023–24): 6 courses. Open a course to see its lecture notes, past exams, homework and projects.

Electromagnetic Wave Theory

Time-harmonic fields and plane waves, lossy media, reflection and refraction (Brewster angle) and waveguides.

3 lecture notes · 1 assignment · 1 project

Communications Laboratory ELM366

Experiments on amplitude modulation (DSB-LC/SC), SSB and FM, and ASK modulation: pre-lab work and reports.

4 labs

Control Systems ELEC322

Mathematical modelling (transfer functions, state space), transient and steady-state analysis, stability, root locus, PID and compensator design.

12 lecture notes · 18 digital notes · 8 exam-prep files · 1 project

Digital Communications ELM365

Sampling and pulse-code modulation, baseband transmission, optimum receivers, digital modulation (ASK, PSK, FSK) and bit-error probability.

14 lecture notes · 14 digital notes · 1 assignment · 1 project

Fundamentals of Digital Signal Processing ELM367

Discrete-time signals and systems, convolution, the z-transform, the discrete Fourier transform and FFT, and digital filter structures.

14 lecture notes · 13 digital notes · 3 assignments

Digital Signal Processing Laboratory ELM368

Weekly DSP exercises in Python/Jupyter (pre-lab work and code) and a walking-vs-running classification project.

6 labs · 1 project
