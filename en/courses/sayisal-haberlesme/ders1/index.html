Sayısal haberleşmenin avantajları

1) Yeniden üretme (Regeneration) sayısal haberleşmenin en iyi avantajı

2) Digital circuits are more reliable, cheaper, more flexible

3) Better Signal processing (SP) removing redundancy → less BW

Sayısal haberleşmenin dezavantajları

1) Intensive SP as compared to analog systems.

2) Synchronization is more complicated as compared to analog systems.

When SNR drops below a certain threshold, the quality of service can suddenly change from very good to very poor. In contrast most analog communication systems degrade gracefully.
