\subsection{5) Peak to Average Ratio}
(tepe gücünün ortalama güce oranı)
\[ \mathrm{PAPR} = \frac{\text{Power of signal when carrier is at }\overbrace{\text{peak}}^{\text{maksimum güç}}}{\text{average power of the carrier}} \]
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{PAPR}_{AM} &= 4{,}26\,\mathrm{dB}\\
\mathrm{PAPR}_{FM} &= 3\,\mathrm{dB}
\end{align*}
