\begin{ornek}
$\eta_T=0{,}5\ \to$ microstrip antenna efficiency\\
$G_T=11\,\mathrm{dB}\ \to$ antenna gain\\
$P_{in}=40\,\mathrm{W}$ (input power before antenna)\\
$T_x$ power density (fall as $1/d^2$)
\end{ornek}

1) $P_{in}=\ldots\ldots$ ?? [dBm]
\[ P_{in\,dBm}=10\log\left(\frac{P_{in}}{10^{-3}}\right)=46\,\mathrm{dBm} \qquad (P_{in}=40) \]
\[ P_{T\,dBm}=P_{in\,dBm}-\underbrace{\eta_{T\,dB}}_{\frac{10\log(0{,}5)}{3\,\mathrm{dB}}} \]

2) $P_T=?$ [dBm]
\[ P_T=P_{in}\cdot\eta_T=40\cdot 0{,}5=20\,\mathrm{W} \]
\[ P_{T\,dBm}=10\log\left(\frac{20}{10^{-3}}\right)=43\,\mathrm{dBm} \]

c) $d=5\,\mathrm{km}\ \to P_D=?$

\elcizimi[0.4]{sekiller/Ders-10-s1-1.png}[1]

\elcizimi[0.6]{sekiller/Ders-10-s1-2.png}[1]
\[ P_D=\frac{P_T\cdot G_T}{4\pi d^2} \]
\[ P_D=\frac{20\cdot 10^{11/10}}{4\pi\,(5\cdot 10^3)^2}=1{,}603\ \mu\mathrm{W/m^2} \]

d) $A_R=6\,\mathrm{cm^2}\ \to P_R=?$
\[ P_R=P_D\cdot A_R=1{,}603\ \mu\mathrm{W/m^2}\cdot(6\,\mathrm{cm^2}) \]
\[ =1{,}603\ \mu\mathrm{W/m^2}\,(6\cdot 10^{-4})\,\mathrm{m^2} \]
\[ =P_R=962\,\mathrm{pW} \]

e) Alıcıdaki gürültü:\\
$N_R=100\,\mathrm{fW}\ \to SNR_R=?$
\[ SNR=\frac{P_R}{N_R}=\frac{962\,\mathrm{pW}}{100\,\mathrm{fW}}=9618\ \to SNR_{dB}=39{,}7\,\mathrm{dB} \]
