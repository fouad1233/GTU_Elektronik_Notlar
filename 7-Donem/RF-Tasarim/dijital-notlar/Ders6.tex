\section{Receive Noise Floor}

Noise floor = Receiver input equivalent noise

$N_o$ : output noise power \underline{exluding} $R_s$.

kaynak \okunamadi{düşünülmediğindeki} gürültü

\begin{align*}
F &= \frac{\overline{v_{n,\mathrm{input}}^2}}{A_{vt}^2}\cdot\frac{1}{4kTR_s} = \frac{\text{Total output noise power including }R_s}{G\cdot(\text{Noise power from }R_s\text{ only})} \\
&= \frac{N_o + G\,(\kutula{kTB})}{G\cdot kTB} = F
\end{align*}

\[ \Rightarrow\; \text{Input noise} = kTB + \frac{N_o}{G} = \underbrace{FkTB}_{\text{Noise Floor}} \]
