\subsection{Small signal model:}
$r_o \rightarrow$ output resistance of bjt \qquad $r_o = \dfrac{|V_A|}{I_C}$

\medskip
$g_m \rightarrow$ transcunductance $= g_m = \dfrac{I_C}{V_T}$ \hfill $\alpha = \dfrac{\beta}{(\beta+1)}$

\medskip
$r_\pi \rightarrow$ base resistance: $r_\pi = (\beta+1)\,r_e$

\medskip
$r_e \rightarrow$ emitter resistance $= \dfrac{V_T}{I_E} = \alpha\,\dfrac{V_T}{I_C} = \alpha\,\dfrac{1}{g_m} \approx \dfrac{1}{g_m}$ \qquad $\underline{\underline{\alpha \approx 1}}$
