\section{Higher order Systems}
\[ \frac{X(s)}{U(s)}=\frac{\overbrace{b_0 s^m+b_1 s^{m-1}+b_2 s^{m-2}+\cdots+b_m}^{\text{zeros of the system}}}{\underbrace{s^n+a_1 s^{n-1}+a_2 s^{n-2}+\cdots+a_n}_{\text{poles of the system}}}\qquad m<n \]
\[ X(s)=\ \text{''}\ U(s) \]
Step inputs are mostly used in analysis.

If the poles are real and distinct
\[ \frac{X(s)}{U(s)}=\frac{r_1}{s+p_1}+\frac{r_2}{s+p_2}+\cdots+\frac{r_n}{s+p_n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{r_i}{s+p_i} \]
\[ \overbrace{r_i}^{\text{residual}}=(s+p_i)\,\frac{X(s)}{U(s)}\Bigg|_{s=-p_i} \]
\[ X(s)=U(s)\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{r_i}{s+p_i} \]
If $U(s)$ is unity (impulse)
\[ \Rightarrow\ x(t)=\sum r_i\,e^{-p_i t} \]
